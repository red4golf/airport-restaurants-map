Airport Restaurants — Fly-In Dining Guide for Pilots

Airport Restaurants is a directory of fly-in dining destinations at general aviation airports across the United States. Browse on-field restaurants, classic $100 hamburger stops, and the best fly-in meals by state and region.

Featured Fly-In Restaurants by State

  • Spruce Goose Café — Jefferson County Intl (0S9), Port Townsend, WA

    On-airport. A legendary fly-in destination since the 1960s. Aviation-themed décor and homemade pies — marionberry and rhubarb are the ones to get. Named a Top 10 Airport Restaurant by AOPA. Watch planes taxi past while you eat. Wed–Sun 8AM–4PM.

  • Amelia's Hangar Restaurant and Lounge — Bremerton National Airport (KPWT), Bremerton, WA

    On-airport. Amelia Earhart-themed restaurant inside a plane hangar at Bremerton National. American classics with generous portions. Sunday brunch buffet draws pilots and locals alike. Tue–Thu 6AM–8PM · Fri–Sat 6AM–9PM · Sun 6AM–8PM · Closed Mon.

  • The Hub - Gig Harbor — Tacoma Narrows Airport (KTIW), Gig Harbor, WA

    On-airport. On-field restaurant right at Tacoma Narrows with runway views. Burgers, pizza, Cajun tots and Harmon craft beers on tap. Watch GA aircraft and business jets roll by. Open daily 11AM–9PM.

  • Ellie's at the Airport — Arlington Municipal Airport (KAWO), Arlington, WA

    On-airport. On-field diner at Arlington Municipal with direct runway views and a devoted local following. Classic American breakfast and lunch — the omelets and French fries draw particular praise. A lively spot with a full bar and a mix of pilots, hangar tenants and locals. Mon–Fri 8AM–2PM · Sat–Sun 8AM–2PM.

  • Flight Deck Restaurant & Lounge — Salem McNary Field (KSLE), Salem, OR

    On-airport. On-field restaurant established in 1968 at Salem McNary Field with windows on three sides giving panoramic views of the runway and Cascade Mountains. Northwest American cuisine with homemade soups, desserts and a full bar. A classic $100 hamburger destination on the I-5 corridor. Mon–Fri 11AM–9PM · Sat–Sun 8AM–9PM.

  • Gaston's Restaurant — Gaston's Airport (3M0), Lakeview, AR

    On-airport. World-famous fly-in fishing resort on the White River. The restaurant serves fresh trout caught steps from the table. One of the most unique fly-in dining experiences in the country — land on the private strip and walk to the table.

  • Flo's Airport Café — Chino Airport (KCNO), Chino, CA

    On-airport. Classic airport diner at Chino Airport — home of the Planes of Fame Air Museum. Breakfast and lunch staples served with a side of warbird sightings. A longtime favorite for pilots flying the Southern California basin.

  • Harris Ranch Restaurant — Harris Ranch Airport (3O8), Coalinga, CA

    On-airport. A legendary California pit stop on the I-5 corridor. Land at the private Harris Ranch strip and walk to one of the state's most famous steakhouses. Prime beef raised on-site. Open for breakfast, lunch and dinner daily.

  • Waypoint Cafe — Camarillo Airport (KCMA), Camarillo, CA

    On-airport. Popular on-field café at Camarillo Airport serving breakfast and lunch. Generous portions and a friendly pilot crowd. On clear days the Channel Islands are visible from the ramp. Open daily 7AM–3PM.

  • The Perfect Landing — Centennial Airport (KAPA), Englewood, CO

    On-airport. Upscale dining with panoramic views of the Front Range and runway at Centennial Airport. One of Colorado's best airport restaurants — think steaks and Colorado craft beers with a view of the Rockies. Open for lunch and dinner.

  • Restaurant 121 — Waterbury-Oxford Airport (KOXC), Oxford, CT

    On-airport. On-field restaurant at Waterbury-Oxford with runway views. Breakfast and lunch served daily. Local favorite for weekend fly-ins in the Connecticut hills. Named for the airport elevation — 726 feet MSL.

  • The Downwind Café — Spruce Creek Fly-In (7FL6), Port Orange, FL

    On-airport. Located inside the famous Spruce Creek Fly-In community — one of the largest residential airparks in the world. Casual café atmosphere with a mix of resident pilots and visiting aviators.

  • CJ Cannon's — Vero Beach Regional Airport (KVRB), Vero Beach, FL

    On-airport. A Vero Beach institution right on the airport with sweeping runway views. Known for generous portions of American comfort food and a lively bar. Popular with both Piper training fleet pilots from next door and transient GA traffic.

  • Flightline Café — Winter Haven Regional Airport (KGIF), Winter Haven, FL

    On-airport. Casual on-field diner at Winter Haven Regional serving breakfast and lunch. Friendly staff and a loyal local pilot following. Good stopping point between Tampa and Orlando in the heart of central Florida's lake country.

  • Runways at Bartow — Bartow Municipal Airport (KBOW), Bartow, FL

    On-airport. On-field restaurant at Bartow Municipal in the heart of Polk County. Breakfast and lunch with views of the ramp and a rotating menu of daily specials. A quiet alternative to busier central Florida airports.

  • Suncoast Café — Venice Municipal Airport (KVNC), Venice, FL

    On-airport. Relaxed café at Venice Municipal — the shark tooth capital of the world. Breakfast and lunch with a friendly airport crowd. Venice has one of the most active GA communities on Florida's Gulf Coast.

  • Tailwind Café — Ocala Intl Jim Taylor Field (KOCF), Ocala, FL

    On-airport. On-field café at Ocala International serving breakfast and lunch to the north-central Florida GA community. Horse country surrounds the airport — a good fuel and food stop between Gainesville and the I-4 corridor.

  • The Hangar Restaurant & Flight Lounge — Albert Whitted Airport (KSPG), St. Petersburg, FL

    On-airport. Waterfront airport restaurant at Albert Whitted with stunning views of Tampa Bay and the St. Pete skyline. One of Florida's most scenic dining spots. Open for breakfast and lunch — arrive early on weekends.

  • Landing Strip Café — Okeechobee County Airport (KOBE), Okeechobee, FL

    On-airport. Down-home Florida diner at Okeechobee County Airport. Breakfast and lunch served to a mix of ag pilots, fishermen and cross-country traffic. Okeechobee sits at the north end of the lake — good stopping point for south Florida trips.

  • Jet Runway Café — Fort Lauderdale Executive Airport (KFXE), Fort Lauderdale, FL

    On-airport. Casual café at Fort Lauderdale Executive — one of the busiest GA airports in the country. Breakfast and lunch with a front-row seat to a parade of jets, turboprops and helicopters.

  • 57th Fighter Group Restaurant — DeKalb-Peachtree Airport (KPDK), Atlanta, GA

    Near airport. WWII-themed restaurant adjacent to DeKalb-Peachtree with a control tower you can dine in. Runway views, wartime memorabilia and a full menu of American classics. One of the most atmospheric airport-adjacent restaurants in the Southeast.

  • Pilot Pete's — Schaumburg Regional Airport (06C), Schaumburg, IL

    On-airport. Beloved on-field diner at Schaumburg Regional in the Chicago suburbs. Big breakfasts, burgers and a loyal pilot crowd. One of the busiest small GA airports in Illinois — expect company on weekend mornings.

  • Rick's Café Boatyard — Eagle Creek Airpark (KEYE), Indianapolis, IN

    Near airport. Waterfront restaurant on Eagle Creek Reservoir adjacent to Eagle Creek Airpark. Seafood, burgers and lake views. A fun combination of the boating and flying communities in one spot on the west side of Indianapolis.

  • The Beaumont Hotel Café — Beaumont Hotel Airport (07S), Beaumont, KS

    On-airport. Historic hotel café at a private Kansas airstrip. The Beaumont Hotel dates to 1879. A genuine step back in time — land at the grass strip and walk to the dining room.

  • Bistro Le Relais — Bowman Field (KLOU), Louisville, KY

    On-airport. French bistro in the historic terminal at Bowman Field — one of the oldest operating airports in the US. White tablecloths and classic French cuisine next to a ramp full of vintage aircraft.

  • Nancy's Air Field Café — Minute Man Air Field (6B6), Stow, MA

    On-airport. Classic New England airport diner at Minute Man Airfield west of Boston. Famous among local pilots for generous breakfasts and friendly service.

  • Crosswinds Restaurant — Nantucket Memorial Airport (KACK), Nantucket, MA

    On-airport. On-field restaurant at Nantucket Memorial with views of one of the most scenic GA airports on the East Coast. Breakfast and lunch.

  • Holman's Table — St. Paul Downtown Airport (KSTP), St. Paul, MN

    On-airport. Upscale farm-to-table dining in the historic Holman Field terminal building overlooking the Mississippi River and downtown St. Paul skyline. One of the most architecturally beautiful airport restaurants in the Midwest.

  • Pik-N-Pig BBQ — Gilliam-McConnell Airfield (5NC3), Carthage, NC

    On-airport. A genuine BBQ landmark accessible only by air or a bumpy dirt road. Fly into this grass strip in the North Carolina Sandhills and taxi up to a pulled pork sandwich that's worth the trip. A bucket-list fly-in for East Coast pilots. Thu–Sun only.

  • Flight Deck Diner — Cape May Airport (KWWD), Cape May, NJ

    On-airport. On-field diner at Cape May Airport on the southern tip of New Jersey. Breakfast and lunch served to a mix of shore-bound vacationers and transient GA traffic.

  • The Apron Cafe — Francis S. Gabreski Airport (KFOK), Westhampton Beach, NY

    On-airport. On-field café at Gabreski Airport on Long Island's East End — gateway to the Hamptons. Breakfast and lunch with ramp views. A popular fuel and food stop for NYC-area pilots heading out to eastern Long Island.

  • Hangars Cafe — Sky Acres Airport (44N), Poughkeepsie, NY

    On-airport. Beloved Hudson Valley airport diner at Sky Acres. Breakfast and lunch served to a tight-knit local flying community. Worth the trip from NYC.

  • Sky Galley — Cincinnati Municipal Lunken Airport (KLUK), Cincinnati, OH

    On-airport. On-field restaurant at Lunken Airport — one of the oldest airports in the country — with art deco terminal views and a classic American menu. Breakfast and lunch. A beloved institution for the Cincinnati aviation community.

  • Tin Goose Diner — Erie-Ottawa International Airport (KPCW), Port Clinton, OH

    On-airport. Named for the Ford Tri-Motor, this diner at Erie-Ottawa International sits on the shores of Lake Erie. Breakfast and lunch with lake views. Port Clinton is the walleye capital of the world — fish specials are a regular feature.

  • Jake's Joint — Ardmore Municipal Airport (KADM), Ardmore, OK

    On-airport. Down-home Oklahoma diner at Ardmore Municipal with a devoted local following. Breakfast and lunch. A good fuel stop on the I-35 corridor between Oklahoma City and Dallas.

  • DeNunzio's Italian Chophouse — Arnold Palmer Regional Airport (KLBE), Latrobe, PA

    On-airport. Upscale Italian steakhouse at Arnold Palmer Regional — fittingly, in the hometown of the King himself. Dinner-focused with a full bar and sweeping views of the Laurel Highlands.

  • Runway Café — Greenville Downtown Airport (KGMU), Greenville, SC

    On-airport. On-field café at Greenville Downtown with ramp views and a loyal local pilot crowd. Breakfast and lunch in the heart of the South Carolina Upstate.

  • Southern Flyer Diner — Brenham Municipal Airport (11R), Brenham, TX

    On-airport. A Texas institution. Chicken fried steak, jalapeño cornbread and sweet tea. Brenham is Blue Bell ice cream country. Breakfast and lunch Fri–Sun. Worth planning a trip around.

  • Hangar 6 Air Café — Garner Field (KUVA), Uvalde, TX

    On-airport. On-field café at Garner Field in the Texas Hill Country. Breakfast and lunch with a strong local ranching and hunting crowd.

  • Hard Eight BBQ — Stephenville Clark Regional Airport (KSEP), Stephenville, TX

    Near airport. One of Texas's most celebrated BBQ joints — brisket, ribs and sausage cooked over mesquite in open pits out front. A short drive from Stephenville Clark Regional in the heart of Cowboy Country. Open daily.

  • Aviator's Grill — David Wayne Hooks Memorial Airport (KDWH), Spring, TX

    On-airport. On-field restaurant at Hooks — one of the busiest GA airports in the Houston metro. Breakfast and lunch with ramp views. A reliable fuel-and-food stop north of Houston.

  • McGehee Catfish Restaurant — McGehee Catfish Restaurant Airport (T40), Cresson, TX

    On-airport. The airport is named after the restaurant — that's how good the catfish is. Land at this Texas private strip and walk to one of the state's most unique dining experiences.

  • Volanti — Scottsdale Airport (KSDL), Scottsdale, AZ

    On-airport. Upscale restaurant at Scottsdale Airport with sweeping desert views and a sleek modern interior. One of the most polished airport dining experiences in the Southwest. Lunch and dinner.

  • Mesa Grill — Sedona Airport (KSEZ), Sedona, AZ

    On-airport. Perched on top of the Sedona Airport mesa at 4,800 feet with arguably the most dramatic views of any airport restaurant in the country. Red rock formations in every direction. Breakfast and lunch.
